How this page works

Getting started

  1. Connect an engine.
  2. Click a point to play.
  3. Turn analysis on.
  4. Add and switch boards.
  5. Players, New game and SGF, the engine console.
  6. Point at a control to learn what it is.

What is drawn on a candidate

  • Two arcs over a track.
  • No blue arc without visits.
  • The white ring on the first candidate.
  • The difference: see Compare two tuples.
